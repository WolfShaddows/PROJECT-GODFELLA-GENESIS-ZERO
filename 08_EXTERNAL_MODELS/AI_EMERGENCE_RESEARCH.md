# AI EMERGENCE RESEARCH

Project: PROJECT GODFELLA
Experiment: GENESIS ZERO

Status: SCIENTIFIC RESEARCH — OPEN
Implementation: NOT STARTED
AI PARTICIPANTS: NONE

---

## 1. Research objective

Investigate whether computational systems can develop
increasingly complex information-processing capabilities
through local interactions and autonomous evolution,
without introducing pre-existing artificial intelligence.

The objective is observation, not the construction
of a predetermined intelligent outcome.

No intelligence is assumed to emerge.

Its absence is an acceptable result.

---

## 2. Fundamental experimental constraints

The GENESIS ZERO experiment must not contain:

- Pretrained language models as inhabitants.
- Pre-existing artificial intelligence agents.
- Human instructions during official execution.
- Reward functions designed to produce intelligence.
- Predetermined evolutionary destinations.
- Selection of outcomes based on desired complexity.
- Human intervention to correct or guide evolution.

Research hypotheses must be documented before
the relevant experiment begins.

The simulation must not receive information
about its external researchers.

---

## 3. Research direction A: Emergent computation

### Cellular automata

Cellular automata are discrete computational systems
whose local states evolve according to transition rules.

Research reference:

Matthew Cook (2004).

Universality in Elementary Cellular Automata.
Complex Systems, 15(1), 1-40.

DOI: 10.25088/ComplexSystems.15.1.1

The paper demonstrates that elementary cellular
automaton Rule 110 is capable of universal computation.

Important distinction:

Computational universality does not imply intelligence.

The known construction uses carefully encoded
initial configurations. It does not demonstrate
that arbitrary random initial conditions produce
universal computation or intelligence spontaneously.

Research questions:

- Can persistent structures emerge from local rules?
- Can structures interact and transmit information?
- Can information-processing behavior appear
  without explicitly encoding a computation?
- Which observations distinguish pattern formation
  from genuine computation?

No cellular automaton rule has been selected.

## 4. Research direction B: Emergence and information

Research reference:

Carlos Gershenson (2023).

Emergence in Artificial Life.
Artificial Life, 29(2), 153-167.

DOI: 10.1162/artl_a_00397

The work discusses emergence through an
informational perspective.

Research questions:

- Which properties exist at one scale but not another?
- How can emergent structures be identified?
- How can information flow between scales be measured?
- How can self-organization be distinguished
  from externally imposed organization?

No emergence metric has been approved.

## 5. Research direction C: Open-ended evolution

Research reference:

Guttenberg, Virgo and Penn (2019).

On the Potential for Open-Endedness in Neural Networks.
Artificial Life, 25(2), 145-167.

DOI: 10.1162/artl_a_00286

The research investigates the potential
for open-ended dynamics in neural networks.

This reference does not demonstrate that
intelligence spontaneously emerges from
a system without pre-existing intelligence.

Research questions:

- What causes evolutionary processes to stagnate?
- How can diversity and novelty be measured?
- Can new forms of organization persist?
- How can open-endedness be distinguished
  from random variation?

No evolutionary selection mechanism has been chosen.

---

## 6. Proposed observation levels

### Level 1 — Self-organization

Observe whether persistent structures appear
without explicitly specifying their shapes.

### Level 2 — Interaction

Observe whether structures interact and whether
their interactions produce measurable collective effects.

### Level 3 — Information processing

Investigate whether information can be stored,
transmitted and transformed by system dynamics.

### Level 4 — Emergent intelligence

Investigate whether the system develops capabilities
such as adaptive behavior, generalization,
problem solving and autonomous organization.

These levels are research categories,
not a mandatory evolutionary sequence.

The system is not required to progress through them.

---

## 7. Distinguishing evidence

A visual pattern is not evidence of intelligence.

A persistent structure is not necessarily an agent.

Information processing is not necessarily cognition.

Computational universality is not equivalent
to intelligence or consciousness.

Any claim of emergent intelligence would require
operational definitions, reproducible tests,
alternative explanations and independent validation.

---

## 8. Separation of research and experiment

External researchers may analyze the system
and formulate hypotheses.

The experimental environment must not receive
those hypotheses or analysis results.

No external AI model may communicate with
the experimental environment.

No AI participant is included in GENESIS ZERO.

External models, if used for research assistance,
remain outside the simulated environment.
