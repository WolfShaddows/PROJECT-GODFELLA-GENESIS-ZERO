## SOURCE POLICY

Every parameter incorporated into the simulation
must reference its source.

Record:

- Publication.
- Dataset version.
- Parameter definition.
- Central value.
- Uncertainty.
- Confidence level.
- Cosmological model.
- Date accessed.

Do not combine measurements from incompatible
models without documenting the consequences.
