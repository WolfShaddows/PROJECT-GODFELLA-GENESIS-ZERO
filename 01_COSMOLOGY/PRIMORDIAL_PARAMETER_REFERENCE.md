## 4. Primordial scalar spectrum

Reference parameterization:

P_R(k) = As * (k/k*)^(ns - 1)

Where:

P_R(k): dimensionless primordial curvature
power spectrum.

As: scalar amplitude at the pivot scale.

k: comoving wavenumber.

k*: pivot wavenumber.

ns: scalar spectral index.

This parameterization assumes a power law without
running of the spectral index.

It is a reference parameterization, not a complete
spatial initial-condition realization.

## 5. Important distinctions

The primordial curvature power spectrum is not
the late-time matter density power spectrum.

A transfer calculation is required to relate
primordial perturbations to later matter perturbations.

The amplitude and spectral index do not uniquely
specify Fourier phases or a particular spatial realization.
