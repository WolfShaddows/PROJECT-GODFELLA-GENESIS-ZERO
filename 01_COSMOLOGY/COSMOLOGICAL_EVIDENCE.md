## 1. COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND

Source:
Planck Collaboration, 2018 Results.

Observational subject:
Temperature and polarization anisotropies
of the cosmic microwave background.

Scientific relevance:
Provides constraints on cosmological parameters
and primordial perturbations.

Important distinction:
Primordial perturbations are inferred through
physical models and comparison with observations.

They are not a direct photograph of the initial universe.

---

## 2. PRIMORDIAL PERTURBATIONS

Reference:
Planck 2018 Results VI.

Scalar spectral index:

ns = 0.965 +/- 0.004

Interpretation:
The measured primordial scalar spectrum is
consistent with a nearly scale-invariant
power-law spectrum under the analyzed model.

This does not establish the ultimate origin
of primordial perturbations.

---

## 3. COSMOLOGICAL PARAMETERS

Reference:
Planck 2018 base Lambda-CDM.

H0 = 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc
Omega_m = 0.315 +/- 0.007
sigma8 = 0.811 +/- 0.006

These are model-dependent inferred parameters.

They are not independent of the cosmological
framework used in the analysis.

---

## 4. BARYON ACOUSTIC OSCILLATIONS

Reference:
DESI cosmological measurements.

Observational subject:
Statistical clustering of galaxies and quasars.

Scientific relevance:
Provides distance-scale information and
constraints on cosmological expansion.

BAO measurements are not direct observations
of primordial density fields.

They provide later-time evidence that can
be compared with cosmological predictions.

---

## 5. EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

Direct observation:
Measured sky maps, spectra and galaxy positions.

Derived measurement:
Statistical quantities extracted from observations.

Model inference:
Cosmological parameters obtained by fitting
observations to a physical model.

Interpretation:
Scientific explanation of the results.

Speculation:
Ideas not established by the available evidence.

These categories must remain separate.

---

## 6. LIMITATIONS

The observations do not establish:

- The ultimate origin of the universe.
- The physical nature of dark matter.
- The physical nature of dark energy.
- The origin of primordial perturbations.
- The existence of life beyond Earth.
