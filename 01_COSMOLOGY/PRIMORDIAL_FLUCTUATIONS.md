## 1. PURPOSE

Study the observational and theoretical basis
of primordial density fluctuations.

No initial perturbation distribution is selected
by this document.

---

## 2. COSMOLOGICAL BACKGROUND

The observable universe exhibits small variations
in temperature and matter distribution.

The cosmic microwave background provides
important observational evidence about
early-universe perturbations.

In the standard cosmological framework,
primordial perturbations provide initial conditions
for subsequent structure formation.

---

## 3. CONCEPTS TO INVESTIGATE

### 3.1 Density Contrast

Definition:

delta(x) = (rho(x) - rho_mean) / rho_mean

Where:

rho(x) = local matter density
rho_mean = mean matter density

### 3.2 Statistical Distribution

- What statistical distributions are supported

### 3.3 Power Spectrum

Questions:

- What is the primordial power spectrum?
- What is its spectral index?
- What observational constraints exist?

### 3.4 Random Phases

Questions:

- How are Fourier phases represented?
- What assumptions are made about their distribution?
- How can a random realization be reproduced?
- How can selection bias be avoided?
