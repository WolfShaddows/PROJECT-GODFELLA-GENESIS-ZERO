# PROJECT GODFELLA
# GENESIS ZERO - PHYSICAL ARCHITECTURE

Version: 0.1
Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL
Experiment: NOT STARTED

---

## 1. OBJECTIVE

Define the physical architecture required to investigate
the emergence of structures from an initially lifeless
cosmological environment.

No biological, intelligent, technological or civilizational
outcome is prescribed.

---

## 2. MODEL LAYERS

### LAYER 1 - COSMOLOGICAL BACKGROUND

Reference model:
Flat Lambda-CDM.

Existing reference engine:
COSMOS ENGINE 0.2.

Parameters:
- Hubble constant.
- Matter density parameter.
- Radiation density parameter.
- Dark energy density parameter.
- Scale factor.
- Cosmological time.

Purpose:
Describe the expansion history of the background universe.

Limitation:
A homogeneous background alone does not generate
gravitational structures.

### LAYER 2 - GRAVITATIONAL STRUCTURE

Proposed physical processes:
- Initial density perturbations.
- Gravitational interaction.
- Matter distribution.
- Structure growth.
- Expansion effects.

Potential numerical approach:
Three-dimensional periodic simulation volume.

Candidate methods:
- Particle-based N-body simulation.
- Particle-mesh gravity.
- Hybrid particle-mesh methods.

No method is selected or frozen yet.

Initial perturbations must be statistically specified
and documented. They must not encode galaxies,
stars, planets or biological structures.

### LAYER 3 - BARYONIC MATTER

Future extension.

Potential processes:
- Gas dynamics.
- Pressure.
- Temperature evolution.
- Shock heating.
- Gravitational collapse.
- Radiative cooling.

This layer is not part of the initial implementation.

### LAYER 4 - CHEMICAL PROCESSES

Future extension.

Potential processes:
- Chemical species.
- Reaction networks.
- Molecular formation.
- Chemical abundance evolution.

No biological processes or organisms will be seeded.

This layer is not part of the initial implementation.

---

## 3. INITIAL CONDITIONS

The initial state must specify:

- Simulation volume.
- Spatial dimensionality.
- Background cosmology.
- Matter components.
- Initial density field.
- Initial velocity field.
- Perturbation spectrum.
- Boundary conditions.
- Numerical resolution.
- Random seed.
- Starting redshift or scale factor.

All parameters must be recorded before execution.

---

## 4. NON-INTERVENTION

After launch:

- No manual modification of the evolving state.
- No insertion of structures.
- No selective removal of results.
- No adjustment intended to produce a desired outcome.

Technical failures may require a separate diagnostic run.
Diagnostic runs must never be confused with the original
experimental run.

---

## 5. VALIDATION REQUIREMENTS

Before the first gravitational simulation:

- Verify numerical stability.
- Verify conservation properties where applicable.
- Compare simplified cases with analytical expectations.
- Test sensitivity to numerical resolution.
- Test reproducibility using recorded random seeds.
- Document numerical errors and limitations.

---

## 6. SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS

This project is a computational model.

It is not a complete reproduction of the physical universe.

Results depend on:
- Chosen physical equations.
- Initial conditions.
- Numerical approximations.
- Resolution.
- Computational limits.

A simulated structure is not automatically evidence
of life, intelligence or consciousness.
