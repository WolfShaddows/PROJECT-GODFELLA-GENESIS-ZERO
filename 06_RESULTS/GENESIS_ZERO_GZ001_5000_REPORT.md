## 7. Entropy methodology

The 5000-cycle analysis used:

- 64 fixed histogram bins.
- Histogram range from 0 to 1.
- Additional underflow and overflow categories.
- The same histogram boundaries for all cycles.

The earlier 500-cycle analysis used histogram
boundaries based on the global minimum and
maximum of that trajectory.

Consequently, the entropy values from these
two analyses are not directly comparable.

The 5000-cycle entropy describes the statistical
distribution of numerical field values.

It is not thermodynamic entropy or a direct
measure of spatial complexity.
