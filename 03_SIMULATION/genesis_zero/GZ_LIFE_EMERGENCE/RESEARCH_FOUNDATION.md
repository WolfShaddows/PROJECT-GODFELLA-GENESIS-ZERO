# GENESIS ZERO — LIFE EMERGENCE RESEARCH

## Research objective

Investigate whether simple deterministic substrates can spontaneously
produce persistent, reproducing, and adaptive structures without
explicitly encoding those structures or objectives.

## Scientific questions

### GZ-LIFE-001 — Structural persistence

Can spatial structures emerge and persist over time?

Measurements:
- Structural lifetime.
- Spatial displacement.
- Shape and pattern conservation.
- Structural transformation.
- Interactions and collisions.
- Appearance and disappearance.

A stationary cell is not necessarily a persistent structure.
Moving structures must also be tracked.

### GZ-LIFE-002 — Reproduction and information transmission

Can a structure produce identifiable descendants?

Required evidence:
- Identifiable parent structure.
- Temporally subsequent offspring.
- Spatially distinguishable offspring.
- Structural similarity or measurable variation.
- Parent-offspring feature correlation.
- Comparison against appropriate null models.

Repeated patterns alone do not establish reproduction.

### GZ-LIFE-003 — Adaptation and organizational maintenance

Can systems maintain or recover organization under changing conditions?

Measurements:
- Perturbation resistance.
- Recovery dynamics.
- Organizational persistence.
- Heritable variation.
- Differential reproduction or survival.
- Changes across generations.

Resilience alone is not evolutionary adaptation.
Adaptation requires evidence of heritable variation
and differential success under environmental conditions.

## Experimental neutrality

- No AI, language models, or autonomous agents inside the substrate.
- No predefined organisms or replicators.
- No reward functions or target outcomes.
- No manual selection of favorable results.
- No communication path between observer and official simulation.
- Preserve all original trajectories and evidence.
- Record failed, negative, and inconclusive results.
- Use independent controls and reproducible configurations.

## Relationship to GZ-EMERGENCE-001

The original 256-rule experiment remains immutable.

Its stored trajectories are retrospective observational material.
They are not to be modified or overwritten.

## Interpretation limits

- Persistence does not establish life.
- Repetition does not establish reproduction.
- Correlation does not establish inheritance.
- Resilience does not establish adaptation.
- Complexity does not establish intelligence.
- Information processing does not establish consciousness.

## Initial execution order

1. GZ-LIFE-001: inspect existing trajectories for structural persistence.
2. GZ-LIFE-002: design and test operational reproduction criteria.
3. GZ-LIFE-003: design controlled perturbation and adaptation experiments.

No experiment is considered successful merely because it produces
visually complex patterns.

The absence of emergence is a valid scientific result.
