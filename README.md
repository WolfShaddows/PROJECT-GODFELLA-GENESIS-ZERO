# PROJECT GODFELLA
## Genesis Zero

Research project:
Artificial Emergence and Cosmological Simulation

### Fundamental hypothesis

Investigate whether increasing complexity, organized structures,
life-like systems and information-processing systems can emerge
from an initially lifeless cosmological environment.

### Initial condition

A primordial state.

No galaxies.
No stars.
No planets.
No organisms.
No biological life.
No intelligence.
No civilizations.
No technology.

### Experimental philosophy

- No seeded organisms.
- No seeded civilizations.
- No artificial intelligence inside the simulated universe.
- No intervention after experimental execution begins.
- All initial conditions must be documented.
- All simulation rules must be reproducible.
- Negative results are valid results.

### Important scientific limitation

This project is a computational model, not a recreation
of the actual universe.

Its conclusions must remain within the limits
of its physical assumptions and computational model.
