# PROJECT GODFELLA
# PRINCIPLE OF NEUTRALITY
# Version 1.0

Status: FUNDAMENTAL EXPERIMENTAL PRINCIPLE
Experiment: NOT STARTED

---

## 1. PURPOSE

The sole objective of GENESIS ZERO is observation.

The experiment has no prescribed destination,
evolutionary objective or desired outcome.

The researchers do not seek to produce a particular
form of complexity.

They seek to observe what follows from the
documented physical rules and initial conditions.

---

## 2. NO PRESCRIBED OUTCOME

The experiment must not contain instructions
designed to produce:

- Galaxies.
- Stars.
- Planets.
- Life.
- Intelligence.
- Consciousness.
- Memory.
- Communication.
- Civilization.
- Technology.
- Transcendence.

Their appearance is neither required nor assumed.

Their absence is equally valid.

---

## 3. RANDOMNESS

Random processes may be used where physically
and mathematically justified.

Random distributions must not be selected
according to the results they produce.

Random seeds and generation procedures
must be recorded.

Randomness must not be confused with
the absence of physical laws.

---

## 4. NON-INTERVENTION

After an experimental run begins:

- No manual alteration of the simulated state.
- No modification of physical parameters.
- No selective removal of outcomes.
- No intervention to encourage complexity.
- No intervention to suppress unexpected behavior.

A technical failure must be documented.

A diagnostic execution must be identified
as a separate run.

---

## 5. OBSERVATION

The observer's role is limited to:

- Recording.
- Measuring.
- Preserving evidence.
- Documenting unexpected events.
- Analyzing completed executions.

Observation must not modify the simulated state.

---

## 6. INTERPRETATION

Every report must distinguish:

1. Direct observation.
2. Measured result.
3. Scientific interpretation.
4. Speculation.

No computational pattern automatically establishes
life, intelligence or consciousness.

Unexpected results must be investigated
without assuming their meaning.

---

## 7. REPRODUCIBILITY

Each run must preserve:

- Model version.
- Physical parameters.
- Initial conditions.
- Random seed or seed-generation procedure.
- Numerical method.
- Software environment.
- Execution configuration.
- Output evidence.

No original run may be silently overwritten.

---

## 8. FUNDAMENTAL DECLARATION

GENESIS ZERO has no programmed destination.

The researchers do not direct its evolution.

They do not demand complexity.

They do not demand simplicity.

They do not demand life.

They do not demand intelligence.

They observe.
