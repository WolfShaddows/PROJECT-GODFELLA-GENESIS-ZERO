# EXPERIMENT RULES

1. No human intervention after launch.
2. No artificial intelligence inserted into the universe.
3. No predefined biological organisms.
4. No predefined civilizations.
5. No predefined technological systems.
6. No manually introduced evolutionary outcomes.
7. Preserve every experimental result.
8. Record failures and unexpected events.
9. Never modify initial conditions during a run.
10. Every experiment must be reproducible.
