# PROJECT GODFELLA
# OBSERVER PROTOCOL 1.0

## PURPOSE

Investigate what structures and forms of complexity
can emerge from an initially lifeless environment.

No particular outcome is assumed.

## FUNDAMENTAL PRINCIPLE

The experiment must not be directed toward a
predetermined evolutionary, biological, technological
or intellectual outcome.

## NON-INTERVENTION

After experimental launch:

1. No human instructions.
2. No human corrections.
3. No human assistance.
4. No manually introduced organisms.
5. No manually introduced intelligence.
6. No manually introduced civilizations.
7. No modification of initial conditions.
8. No selective deletion of unfavorable results.

## EMERGENCE

The simulation must not prescribe:

- Life.
- Intelligence.
- Consciousness.
- Memory.
- Communication.
- Civilization.
- Technology.
- Transcendence.

The environment may permit physical processes
and persistent state changes.

Any resulting structures must emerge from
the implemented rules.

## OBSERVATION

Human observers may:

- Record events.
- Preserve evidence.
- Measure variables.
- Analyze results after execution.
- Document unexpected behavior.

Observers may not influence the running experiment.

## SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

Absence of emergence is a valid result.

Unexpected outcomes must not be discarded.

Computational behavior must not automatically
be interpreted as biological life or consciousness.

Every conclusion must distinguish observation,
interpretation and speculation.
