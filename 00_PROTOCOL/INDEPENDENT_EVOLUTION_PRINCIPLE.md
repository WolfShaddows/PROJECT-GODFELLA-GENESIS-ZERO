# INDEPENDENT EVOLUTION PRINCIPLE

Project: PROJECT GODFELLA
Experiment: GENESIS ZERO

Status: FOUNDATIONAL METHODOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE — APPROVED
Execution: NOT STARTED

## 1. Fundamental principle

After an official run begins, the simulated
universe must evolve exclusively according to
its previously documented initial conditions
and computational rules.

Human researchers must not direct its evolution.

The system has no predefined destination.

## 2. No communication with researchers

During an official run, the simulated universe
must not receive:

- Human instructions.
- Human corrections.
- Human-selected outcomes.
- External evolutionary guidance.
- Feedback based on observed behavior.

No communication channel from the research
environment to the simulated universe is permitted.

## 3. No human intervention

Researchers must not modify the state,
parameters, rules, or internal configuration
during an official run.

Unexpected behavior must not be corrected.

Unwanted outcomes must not be removed.

No outcome may be selected or rewarded.

## 4. Independent execution

The simulation must be capable of continuing
without an active human session.

Its execution must not depend on researchers
watching the system.

Operational limits, hardware constraints,
and predetermined stopping conditions must
be documented before launch.

A technical interruption must be recorded
as an interruption, not silently treated
as a natural evolutionary event.

## 5. External observation

Observation and evolution must be separated
into independent components.

The observation component may:

- Read authorized state information.
- Record measurements.
- Preserve timestamps and execution metadata.
- Store events and checkpoints.
- Report technical failures.

It must not:

- Modify the simulated state.
- Send instructions to the simulation.
- Change physical parameters.
- Select evolutionary outcomes.
- Alter execution rules based on observations.

## 6. Observation interference audit

The observation mechanism may introduce
computational overhead or other indirect effects.

Before an official run, its influence must
be measured and documented.

Where possible, observation should be read-only.

Any unavoidable measurement overhead must
be disclosed.

## 7. Researcher responsibilities

Researchers may formulate hypotheses
before an official run.

Predictions must be timestamped and preserved
before results are examined.

After a run, researchers may analyze,
compare, interpret, and publish observations.

Post-run analysis must never modify
the preserved original run.

## 8. Unknown outcomes

The system is not required to produce
complexity, structures, life, intelligence,
consciousness, or any other desired outcome.

Stability, transformation, disorder,
termination, and absence of emergence
are all valid possible observations.

No result is guaranteed.

## 9. Operational integrity

Each official run must preserve:

- Initial configuration.
- Model version.
- Rule definitions.
- Random seed, if applicable.
- Execution metadata.
- Observation records.
- Interruptions and failures.
- Final state, when available.

Original evidence must remain immutable.

## 10. Approved principle

NO DESTINATION.
NO HUMAN INTERVENTION.
NO EVOLUTIONARY GUIDANCE.
ONLY INDEPENDENT EXECUTION AND OBSERVATION.

This principle establishes a methodological
requirement. It does not imply consciousness,
free will, or autonomous intelligence.
