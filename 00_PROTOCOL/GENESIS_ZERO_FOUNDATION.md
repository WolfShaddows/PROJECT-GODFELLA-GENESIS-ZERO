# GENESIS ZERO — FOUNDATIONAL DECISION

Project: PROJECT GODFELLA
Experiment: GENESIS ZERO

Status: FOUNDATIONAL DECISION — APPROVED
Execution: NOT STARTED

## 1. Origin concept

GENESIS ZERO begins from an abstract computational
initial state.

The initial state does not claim to represent:

- The Big Bang.
- A cosmological singularity.
- A known physical origin of reality.
- A specific inflationary epoch.
- A reconstructed version of our observable universe.

No claim is made about what preceded the initial state.

## 2. Experimental philosophy

The experiment has no predefined destination.

It does not seek to produce:

- Galaxies.
- Stars.
- Planets.
- Biological life.
- Intelligence.
- Civilizations.
- Technology.
- Consciousness.

None of these outcomes will be inserted,
required, rewarded, or selected.

The experiment exists to observe what follows
from its explicitly documented initial state
and computational rules.

## 3. Unknown physical phenomena

Unknown phenomena will not be silently converted
into established physical laws.

Dark matter, quantum gravity, and other unresolved
physical questions will not be introduced as
established facts.

Any future hypothetical model must be explicitly
identified as hypothetical and separately documented.

## 4. Initial-state neutrality

The initial state will be defined through
documented computational properties.

No property will be selected because it is expected
to produce a desired evolutionary outcome.

The absence of emergence is a valid result.

Unexpected outcomes must be preserved and examined,
not corrected to fit expectations.

## 5. Physical and computational transparency

Every implemented rule must be classified as one of:

- Established physical principle.
- Empirical approximation.
- Numerical method.
- Explicit experimental hypothesis.
- Unresolved or unknown.

The computational model must not be presented
as a complete reproduction of physical reality.

## 6. Observation policy

Human intervention after the official launch
is prohibited.

No manual correction, insertion, removal,
selection, or assistance will be permitted
during an official run.

Observation and evidence preservation remain
the human responsibilities.

## 7. Current limitations

The abstract initial state has not yet been
numerically defined.

No spatial configuration has been generated.

No random seed has been generated.

No final physical rule set has been approved.

No simulation has been launched.

## 8. Foundational decision

GENESIS ZERO will begin from an abstract
computational initial state.

It will not claim to reproduce the Big Bang
or the absolute origin of the universe.

This decision establishes the experimental
framework, not the numerical initial conditions.

The experiment remains unlaunched.
