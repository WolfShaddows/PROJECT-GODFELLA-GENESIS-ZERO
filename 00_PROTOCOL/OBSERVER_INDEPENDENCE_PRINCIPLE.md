# OBSERVER INDEPENDENCE PRINCIPLE

Project: PROJECT GODFELLA
Experiment: GENESIS ZERO

Status: FOUNDATIONAL METHODOLOGICAL PRINCIPLE — APPROVED
Execution: NOT STARTED

## 1. Fundamental principle

The existence of the experimental universe
does not depend on the presence of an internal
observer.

GENESIS ZERO does not require consciousness,
intelligence, life, or self-awareness to exist
or evolve.

## 2. Internal observers

No internal observer will be inserted.

No conscious entity will be programmed.

No artificial intelligence will participate
as an inhabitant of the experimental universe.

The absence of internal observers is not
considered a failure or an incomplete outcome.

## 3. External observation

Human researchers will observe the experiment
from outside its modeled universe.

Observation is intended to record and analyze
the behavior of the system.

The observer does not define the existence
of the system being observed.

## 4. Observation and measurement

Observation and measurement must be distinguished.

The recording mechanism may introduce
computational overhead or unintended effects.

Such effects must be investigated and documented.

The observation system must not introduce
evolutionary instructions, desired outcomes,
or artificial selection.

Where possible, observation must be performed
without modifying the simulated state.

## 5. Absence of observation

An event not recorded by the observation system
cannot automatically be classified as an event
that did not occur.

Absence of evidence must not automatically
be interpreted as evidence of absence.

Detection limits must be documented.

## 6. Scientific distinction

This principle is a methodological rule
for GENESIS ZERO.

It does not claim to resolve:

- The quantum measurement problem.
- The role of observers in quantum mechanics.
- The fundamental nature of consciousness.
- The ultimate nature of physical reality.

No interpretation of quantum mechanics
is selected by this document.

## 7. Experimental consequences

The universe may evolve without internal observers.

No observer must appear.

No consciousness must emerge.

No self-awareness must develop.

The absence of these phenomena is a valid result.

## 8. Approved principle

GENESIS ZERO does not require the universe
to observe itself in order to exist
within the experimental model.

Observation is an external research activity,
not a predefined condition of existence.

## 9. Project status

Principle: APPROVED.

Internal observers: NOT INTRODUCED.

External observation: REQUIRED FOR RESEARCH.

Observation interference: MUST BE AUDITED.

Consciousness: NOT PRESCRIBED.

Simulation: NOT LAUNCHED.
